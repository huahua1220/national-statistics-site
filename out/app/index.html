The National Ledger United States in figures

A plain-language almanac of U.S. statistics — every figure with its source and year.

The National Ledger is an independent, unofficial reference compiled from public releases of the U.S. Census Bureau, Bureau of Economic Analysis, Bureau of Labor Statistics, Federal Reserve, Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, National Center for Education Statistics, U.S. Treasury and other named agencies. It is not affiliated with the U.S. Government.

Figures are the most recent annual or recent-month readings at compilation; each is labelled with its source and year. Charts are drawn by hand in this site's own SVG code — no chart library, nothing fetched over the network.
